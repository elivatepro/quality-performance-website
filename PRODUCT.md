# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Dealership decision-makers: general managers, F&I directors, fixed-ops directors at franchise and independent dealerships in the Northeast US. They evaluate vendors between customer conversations, often on a phone from the showroom floor. Their job: protect lot inventory, add F&I revenue without adding overhead, and avoid vendors who create work. Secondary: individual car owners referred by a dealer.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Quality Performance, a multi-state PPF (paint protection film) installation company. The site's job is to generate qualified dealership partnership leads and route existing partners to the operations portal (app.qualityperformance.io). Success = a GM trusts us enough to book a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Confident, physical, meticulous. The brand voice of a master installer: plain sentences, no hype verbs, specifics over superlatives. Cinematic-automotive visual language: photography-led, dark, engineered. Should evoke the feeling of a premium vehicle reveal, not a SaaS product launch.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI landing pages: eyebrow label over every section, three equal feature cards, centered everything.
- SaaS gradient-glow aesthetics; this is a company that works with its hands on real cars.
- Cheap tint-shop websites: neon accents, aggressive ALL CAPS, cluttered promos.
- Fake dashboards built from styled divs.

## Design Principles

1. **The car is the hero.** Photography and physical detail carry sections; type supports.
2. **Show the work, not the claims.** Real install points, real process, real coverage.
3. **One idea per fold.** Long scroll, deliberate pacing, generous space.
4. **Engineered precision.** Alignment, tabular numbers, and consistent hardware-like details signal install quality.
5. **Quiet confidence.** No exclamation marks, no hype verbs, restrained motion that rewards attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark palette (verify blue-on-navy pairings). Full keyboard navigation with visible focus rings. All motion honors prefers-reduced-motion. Meaningful alt text on all imagery.
